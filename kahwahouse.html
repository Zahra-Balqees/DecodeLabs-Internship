our story

About Kahwa House

Kahwa House opened in 2021 as a two-table nook with one roaster and a stubborn belief: coffee should taste like it was made by someone who cares. Five years on, that hasn't changed.

Every bean is sourced from small growers, roasted in small batches, and pulled to order — never sitting, never rushed. The maroon booths and cream walls are meant to feel like someone's front room, because that's exactly what we wanted to build.
